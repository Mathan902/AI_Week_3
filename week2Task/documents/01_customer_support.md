# Customer Support: Delivery and Returns Addendum

## Damaged parcels
Customers must report a parcel that arrived damaged within 72 hours of delivery. The report must include the order number and at least two clear photographs showing the damaged item and the outer packaging. Support acknowledges a complete report within one business day.

## Returns
Unopened standard products may be returned within 30 calendar days. Personalized products cannot be returned unless they are defective. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within five to seven business days after inspection.
