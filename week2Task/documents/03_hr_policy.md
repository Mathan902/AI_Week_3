# HR Policy: Flexible Work Addendum

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed 90 days of service may request hybrid work. An approved employee may work remotely for up to two days per workweek. Tuesday is the required on-site collaboration day for all participating employees.

## Requests and equipment
Requests must be submitted to the line manager at least five business days before the proposed start date. The company supplies one monitor and one keyboard. Employees remain responsible for a suitable internet connection and a private workspace.
