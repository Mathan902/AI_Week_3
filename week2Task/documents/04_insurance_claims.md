# Home Insurance: Water Damage Endorsement

## Immediate duties
The policyholder must take reasonable steps to stop additional water damage when it is safe to do so. The loss must be reported within five calendar days of discovery. Photographs of affected rooms and damaged property must be submitted within ten calendar days of the initial claim report.

## Coverage limits
Emergency plumbing needed to stop an active leak is covered up to 1,500 currency units. Gradual seepage lasting more than 14 days and damage caused by poor maintenance are excluded.
