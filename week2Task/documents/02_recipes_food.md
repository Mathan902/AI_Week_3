# Weeknight Cookbook: Lentil Stew

## Ingredients and method
Simmer one cup of red lentils with four cups of vegetable stock, one chopped tomato, and one teaspoon of cumin for 25 minutes. Add one teaspoon of chilli flakes during the final five minutes. Salt only after the lentils become tender.

## Adjustments
For a milder stew, omit the chilli flakes and stir in half a cup of coconut milk. For a thicker result, rest the finished stew uncovered for ten minutes. The recipe serves four and keeps refrigerated for three days.
